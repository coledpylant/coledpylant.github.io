Public Safety & Crime Analytics

A collection of four Tableau dashboards built from public datasets to compare crime, incarceration, and traffic-safety patterns across place and time.

What this work demonstrates

These dashboards emphasize data normalization, geographic comparison, time trends, seasonality, interactive filtering, and communicating complex public datasets in a form that can be scanned quickly.
